Frequently asked questions

Everything commanders and platform owners ask before rollout.

How does Continuum detect an incident before customers notice?

Continuum ingests every monitoring and alerting feed you already run, ranks incoming signals by severity and blast radius, and surfaces the top of the queue automatically — no manual triage required.

Can multiple teams coordinate on the same incident?

Yes. Each incident gets an assigned lead, a live responder roster, and a shared timeline, so infrastructure, network, and database teams all work from the same picture.

What happens after the incident is resolved?

Every action taken during response is timestamped automatically, giving you a ready-made postmortem instead of a manual writeup.

Does Continuum work across regions and cloud providers?

Continuum tracks resource capacity and response status across every region and provider you operate in, from a single command view.
